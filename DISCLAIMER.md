# Important limitations

劳动信息检索 is a research aid. **It is not a blacklist and is not legal advice.** It does not rate companies, decide whether a company is good or bad, or recommend whether you should buy from one.

Search coverage is incomplete and depends on public search results, accessible pages, and the names and relationships you confirm. A missing result does not show that no issue exists. A result, complaint, or allegation is not by itself a verified finding. Read the original source, date, context, company response, corrections, and later developments before drawing a conclusion.

Brand owners, sellers, manufacturers, principals, and subcontractors can be different organizations. A name match is not authoritative identity verification. Treat uncertain matches cautiously and correct the subject before searching. Links that require login, CAPTCHA, dynamic rendering, or blocked access may not parse; the app will offer manual or screenshot input where possible.
