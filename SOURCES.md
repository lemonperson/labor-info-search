# Sources and coverage

The app searches publicly accessible information and presents it with source links and context. Search coverage is incomplete and depends on upstream availability, names, platform behavior, and public indexing. A source label is a classification aid, not a statement that every page from a domain is authoritative.

## Runtime search

| Source | Use | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bing RSS (`bing-rss`) | Public web-search results for the confirmed company/keyword and query terms | Search index coverage and ranking are controlled by Bing; RSS availability and result formats can change. |
| Taobao, JD, Douyin, Pinduoduo public product pages | Best-effort extraction of visible product metadata and business/manufacturer labels from a link the user supplies | Dynamic pages, login, CAPTCHA, redirects, anti-automation, and format changes may prevent extraction. No access control is bypassed. |

The source registry also classifies result domains for government, court/arbitration, and media links. Classification does not mean those sites are queried comprehensively or that a result is independently verified. Search follows publicly available pages and the current registry; it does not provide exhaustive coverage of those sites.

## Review each result

Follow the original link and check publisher, publication date, subject identity, context, company response, correction, legal or regulatory outcome, and later developments. Personal statements and complaints remain attributed claims. Do not treat a name match, a search snippet, or an absence of results as proof.

## Corrections

Use the **Source correction** issue form to report a broken link, misclassified source, or missing public follow-up. Include the public URL and the specific correction requested. Do not submit private personal data.
